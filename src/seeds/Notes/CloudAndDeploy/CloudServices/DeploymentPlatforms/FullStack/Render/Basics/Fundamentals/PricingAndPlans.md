# Pricing & Plans

<hr class="dividerSection" />

## Overview

<hr class="dividerSection" />

Render offers four pricing tiers ranging from a free Hobby plan for personal projects to Enterprise for mission critical applications.

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>All plans include compute costs on top of the base plan price.</li>
    <li>No credit card is required to use the free Hobby tier.</li>
    <li>Static sites are permanently free with no spin-down behavior.</li>
    <li>Free web services spin down after 15 minutes of inactivity with cold starts taking 30 to 60 seconds.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## Hobby Plan (Free)

<hr class="dividerSection" />

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>A workspace just for you.</li>
    <li>5 GB of bandwidth included ($0.15/GB after).</li>
    <li>Unlimited domains.</li>
    <li>500 build minutes.</li>
    <li>Unlimited projects with 2 environments.</li>
    <li>Deploy up to 25 services.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<hr class="dividerSection" />

## Pro Plan ($25/mo)

<hr class="dividerSection" />

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>Unlimited team members.</li>
    <li>25 GB of bandwidth included ($0.15/GB after).</li>
    <li>15 custom domains ($0.25/domain after).</li>
    <li>1000 build minutes.</li>
    <li>Unlimited projects and environments.</li>
    <li>Deploy unlimited services.</li>
    <li>Horizontal autoscaling.</li>
    <li>Performance builds.</li>
    <li>Preview and isolated environments.</li>
    <li>Workspace audit logs.</li>
    <li>SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 compliance.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<hr class="dividerSection" />

## Scale Plan ($499/mo)

<hr class="dividerSection" />

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>Everything in Pro plus:</li>
    <li>1 TB of bandwidth ($0.15/GB after).</li>
    <li>25 custom domains ($0.25/domain after).</li>
    <li>5000 build minutes.</li>
    <li>Workspace and organization audit logs.</li>
    <li>SAML SSO, SCIM, and role-based access.</li>
    <li>HIPAA compliance (compute-based cost).</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<hr class="dividerSection" />

## Enterprise Plan (Custom)

<hr class="dividerSection" />

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>Everything in Scale plus:</li>
    <li>Custom contracts.</li>
    <li>Dedicated support.</li>
    <li>Custom SLAs.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
