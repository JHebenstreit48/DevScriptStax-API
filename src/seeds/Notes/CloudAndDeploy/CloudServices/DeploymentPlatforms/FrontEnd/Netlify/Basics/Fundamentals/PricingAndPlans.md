# Choosing a Netlify Plan

<hr class="dividerSection" />

Netlify offers three pricing tiers, from a free plan for personal projects to custom Enterprise pricing for large-scale needs.

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>Netlify's paid plans are priced using a monthly credits system rather than flat bandwidth limits.</li>
    <li>The Free plan includes 300 credits per month at no cost, with no overage charges.</li>
    <li>The Pro plan starts with 3,000 credits per month, and the credit allotment can be adjusted.</li>
    <li>Enterprise plans use fully custom pricing based on organization needs.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## Free Plan ($0)

<hr class="dividerSection" />

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>Deploy from AI, Git, or API.</li>
    <li>Custom domains with SSL.</li>
    <li>Unlimited deploy previews.</li>
    <li>Run tasks with AI agents.</li>
    <li>Serverless functions and storage.</li>
    <li>Global CDN.</li>
    <li>Observability with custom queries and 24-hour data retention.</li>
    <li>300 credits per month included, with no overage charges.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<hr class="dividerSection" />

## Pro Plan ($20/mo)

<hr class="dividerSection" />

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>3,000 credits per month included, adjustable to fit team needs.</li>
    <li>Use Agent Runners.</li>
    <li>Private organization repos.</li>
    <li>Shared environment variables.</li>
    <li>3+ concurrent builds.</li>
    <li>Password protected projects.</li>
    <li>Custom identity emails, templates, and audit log.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<hr class="dividerSection" />

## Enterprise Plan (Custom)

<hr class="dividerSection" />

<div class="centeredBullet">
  <ul class="diamondBullets fullWidthBullet">
    <li>99.99% uptime SLA.</li>
    <li>Enterprise network tier.</li>
    <li>High-performance builds.</li>
    <li>SSO and SCIM.</li>
    <li>Log drains.</li>
    <li>Organization management.</li>
    <li>24/7 support.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
